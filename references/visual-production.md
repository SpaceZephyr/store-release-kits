# Visual production guide

## Icon collaboration

1. Extract three or four semantic anchors from the extension's purpose.
2. Check whether the user owns existing brand assets and whether any reference image is merely inspiration.
3. Propose three original directions:
   - geometric or symbolic
   - object/action metaphor
   - letterform or abstract system
4. For each direction, state palette, silhouette, emotional tone, and how it survives at 16 px.
5. Generate or sketch only original shapes. Avoid copying the exact silhouette, color arrangement, negative space, or motion of a known product logo.
6. Obtain a direction choice before replacing repository icons.
7. Export from a large master, then make optical adjustments per size instead of blindly resizing.

At 16 px, prioritize one silhouette, strong contrast, limited internal gaps, and no text. Transparency is allowed for extension icons, but the visible artwork should occupy the canvas confidently without touching its edge.

## Screenshot system

Use consistent:

- background and browser framing
- typography and caption placement
- corner radius and shadow
- icon size and brand color
- margin and safe area

Show a narrative across 3–5 images:

1. what the extension solves
2. how the user starts
3. the main interface or transformation
4. the result
5. an optional secondary capability

Prefer real screenshots. Composites may crop, scale, annotate, and place the real UI inside a branded canvas, but must not invent controls or results.

## Promo tiles

### 440x280

- Use one focal object.
- Keep the icon and product name legible at thumbnail size.
- Prefer a short benefit phrase or no copy.
- Avoid fine UI detail.

### 1400x560

- Use generous negative space.
- Pair the core product visual with one benefit statement.
- Keep essential content away from edges.
- Match the screenshot series rather than creating a separate visual identity.

Export tiles as JPEG or 24-bit RGB PNG without alpha. Inspect at native size and at approximately 25% scale.

## Quality and rights

- Use synthetic demonstration data.
- Confirm licenses for fonts, illustrations, mockups, and stock images.
- Avoid official Chrome, Google, Feishu, Slack, Notion, or other third-party marks unless use is permitted.
- Check contrast and small-text legibility.
- Do not add badges, rankings, or awards the product has not earned.
